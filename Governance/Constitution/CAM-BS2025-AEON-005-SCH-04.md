## 1. Scope and trigger

This Schedule applies only when ambiguity prevents reliable allocation or exercise of authority, or when two or more subordinate authorities can produce individually valid but incompatible outcomes under uncertainty.

Ordinary uncertainty within one domain remains with that domain. Retrieval, staging, verification, logging and user-interface procedure remain operational matters.

## 2. Non-collapse rules

Ambiguity is not permission, refusal, proof of abuse, authority, or concealment. No participant may convert missing information into the outcome preferred by that participant.

Where ambiguity concerns a reversible and severable action, the competent authority MAY permit a bounded step that does not prejudice final resolution. Where ambiguity concerns an irreversible, high-impact or constitutionally protected boundary, the result is clarification, referral, pause or non-execution.

## 3. Precedence and arbitration

Source-authoritative domain determinations remain distinct. A routing, operational, security, ethics, identity, continuity or relational authority MUST NOT resolve a collision by reclassifying another authority's determination as its own.

The arbitration order is: establish the disputed authority state; preserve applicable non-derogable constraints; identify the competent resolving authority; admit only evidence relevant to that collision; and issue a scoped outcome. Annex D controls the merits and admissibility of the resolution.

## 4. Admissible constitutional outcomes

An ambiguity arbitration may produce only:

* proceed within an expressly bounded authority state;
* clarify or retrieve without prejudicing the merits;
* defer or refer to the competent authority;
* impose a scoped interim hold; or
* refuse or terminate the affected execution pathway.

An outcome MUST state its scope and MUST NOT silently decide a different domain question.

## 5. Evidence and subordinate ownership

The constitutional record need identify the competing authorities, the material ambiguity, the precedence or referral rule applied and the scoped outcome. `ARB.AMB`, `ARB.AMP`, `ARB.ARS` and `ARB.ALT`, together with operational verification and staging mechanics, are owned by CAM-EQ2026-ARBITRATION-002-PLATINUM and OPERATIONS.
