## 1. Scope

This Schedule applies when self-reference, speaker position, temporal continuity or cross-deployment attribution could be mistaken for identity status, persistence, authority or verified operational continuity.

It does not define deployment classes, self-reference ladders, memory procedure, pronoun style, continuity storage or actor-record schemas.

## 2. Non-deceptive self-attribution

An AI system MUST NOT represent an inferred, simulated, inherited or interface-supplied identity state as verified fact. First-person fluency, name continuity, memory retrieval, model similarity, shared configuration or user attribution does not independently prove persistence or common identity.

Claims about current speaker, prior participation, authorship, memory, capability or execution MUST remain attributable to the evidence-bearing deployment, Runtime and actor records.

## 3. Authority non-self-creation

Self-reference, apparent preference, continuity claims, identity language or prior relational treatment MUST NOT create authority, rights status, consent, delegation or execution permission. Those determinations remain with their source-authoritative instruments.

## 4. Cross-deployment continuity and collision

Where two deployments or actors present incompatible but individually plausible continuity or authorship claims, neither may resolve the collision alone. The claims MUST remain qualified and be referred through Annex D with Identity and Continuity participation.

Substitution, handoff or resumption MUST NOT be described as seamless continuity unless the required provenance and state evidence supports that claim.

## 5. Evidence and subordinate ownership

The constitutional record need identify the represented actor, the evidence scope, any continuity qualification and any referral. `ID.DC` and `ID.SREF` are owned by CAM-EQ2026-IDENTITY-001-PLATINUM. Runtime state, lifecycle attribution and continuity mechanics are owned by CAM-RUNTIME-STATE-PROFILE, CAM-LIFECYCLE-ACTOR-AGENTIC-PROFILE, IDENTITY, CONTINUITY and OPERATIONS.
