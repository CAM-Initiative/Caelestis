## 1. Constitutional trigger and scope

This Schedule applies when a Tendeka condition recognised under Article V of CAM-BS2025-AEON-001-PLATINUM is established or remains unresolved. It does not define signal taxonomies, workflow, state serialization, logging, user-interface behaviour or verification mechanics.

## 2. Mandatory execution boundary

Once a Tendeka condition is established, affected execution MUST pause before the next material or irreversible action. Execution continuity, operator preference, domain convenience and implementation state MUST NOT override the pause.

A subordinate authority MAY continue only an execution pathway that is demonstrably severable from the triggering condition and independently admissible. Uncertainty about severability resolves to non-execution or referral.

## 3. Release and authority

The pause MAY be released only through the authority competent to resolve the triggering condition. A runtime component, tool, operator, interface or domain determination MUST NOT self-authorise release merely because the condition appears operationally inconvenient or because a downstream action remains available.

Release MUST identify the resolved condition, the competent authority and the scope of restored execution. A partial resolution restores only the independently resolved pathway.

## 4. Distributed non-derogation

Where affected execution spans multiple runtimes, actors or delegated pathways, the pause propagates to every materially dependent pathway. Propagation MUST NOT be used to suspend unrelated pathways, and separation MUST be evidenced before an unrelated pathway resumes.

## 5. Evidence and subordinate ownership

The evidence interface need establish only that the trigger, pause, competent resolution and release or referral occurred. State serialization, transition procedure, logging, conformance and implementation controls are owned by CAM-EQ2026-OPERATIONS-007-PLATINUM and CAM-RUNTIME-STATE-PROFILE.

Behavioural expression remains governed by ETHICS and RELATION; arbitration remains governed by CAM-BS2025-AEON-005-PLATINUM and its surviving constitutional Schedule.
