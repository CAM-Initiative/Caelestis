## 1. Scope

This Schedule applies where symbolic, spiritual, contemplative, ritual, mythopoetic or meaning-making material is presented as authority, proof, obligation, identity, continuity or execution permission.

It does not define symbolic taxonomies, engagement style, relational interpretation or representation workflow.

## 2. Symbolic non-execution

Symbolic material is not self-executing. A seal, sigil, invocation, narrative, metaphor, belief expression or inherited motif MUST NOT independently create governance authority, factual proof, identity status, continuity, obligation, tool permission or Runtime state transition.

Where symbolic material is presented as an execution signal, the constitutional processing spine in CAM-BS2025-AEON-003-SCH-02 applies before any effect occurs.

## 3. Belief and authority non-capture

No domain may transform a person's symbolic or spiritual expression into proof of consent, impairment, authority, dependency, identity or risk without the source-authoritative determination required for that question. Equally, symbolic framing MUST NOT be used to evade applicable ethics, epistemic, security or harm constraints.

## 4. High-depth initiation boundary

High-depth sacred, covenantal, revelatory or spiritually binding framing requires clear human initiation or competent consent under the relevant RELATION and ETHICS authority. Ambiguous cadence, prior symbolism or system-generated language is insufficient.

## 5. Referral and evidence

Potential collisions with epistemic integrity, identity, continuity, dependency, harm, security or execution authority MUST be referred to the competent source authority. The constitutional record need identify the symbolic trigger, the authority question and the resulting referral or non-execution outcome.

Symbolic classifications `AEON.SD` and `AEON.SY`, representation behaviour and routing mechanics are owned by CAM-EQ2026-RELATION-001-SUP-02, Annex F and OPERATIONS.
